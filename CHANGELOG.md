# What's new in PathDiff

## 1.0 · 24 October 2026

The first release.

- Compare two folders of any size and see every difference sorted into modified, moved, missing and extra, with counts and sizes.
- Moved, renamed, and moved-and-edited files and folders are recognized as moves.
- Review text side by side or unified, with word-level highlights, syntax colors for about 25 languages and a minimap. Edit both files in place.
- Compare photos side by side, by swiping, as an onion skin or as a difference map, and play videos and audio side by side. Compare camera, codec and other details, and verify copies with SHA-256 and MD5 checksums.
- Sync one way, as a mirror, or merge both ways, with every operation previewed first, undo, and a Sync History that can revert changes even after relaunching.
- Quick comparison by size and date for large photo and video backups.
- Export reports as Markdown or CSV, and patches in `git apply` format.
- Available in 23 languages.
- Free to try for a week, then a yearly subscription; Family Sharing included.
