# Support

[← Back to PathDiff](README.md)

## Help inside the app

Choose **Help ▸ PathDiff Help** (⌘?) for a short guide to comparing, reviewing and syncing. Answers to common questions are in the [FAQ](docs/FAQ.md).

## Report a bug, ask for a feature, or send feedback

[Open an issue](https://github.com/OpenAppBox/PathDiff/issues/new/choose) and pick a template:

- **Bug**: something doesn't work as expected.
- **Feature request**: something you wish PathDiff did.
- **Feedback**: questions, translation suggestions, anything else.

Before opening a new issue, please [search the existing ones](https://github.com/OpenAppBox/PathDiff/issues) and add your details there if someone already reported it.

### What helps us fix a bug

- Your PathDiff version (**PathDiff ▸ About PathDiff**) and macOS version (Apple menu ▸ About This Mac).
- What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead.
- What kind of folders you compared: roughly how many files, which kinds (code, photos, videos), and where they are (internal disk, external drive, network share).
- A screenshot, if it shows the problem.

Issues are public. Please don't attach private files, and don't include personal information or folder names you'd rather keep private.

### Diagnostic logs

For problems with scanning or syncing, logs help. Open **Console** (in Applications ▸ Utilities), start streaming, search for `com.openappbox.pathdiff`, reproduce the problem, and copy the lines that appear. Check them for private file names before posting.

## Security issues

Please don't report security problems in a public issue. See [Security](SECURITY.md).

## Purchases and refunds

PathDiff's subscription is sold through the Mac App Store, so Apple handles purchases, payments and refunds.

- **Manage or cancel** the subscription: **PathDiff ▸ Settings ▸ General ▸ Manage Subscription…**, or your App Store account settings.
- **Subscribed but PathDiff still shows the offer?** Click **Restore Purchases** in the offer.
- **Refunds and purchase questions:** [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).
