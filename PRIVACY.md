# PathDiff privacy policy

*Last updated: [DATE]*

PathDiff is a Mac app that compares and syncs folders on your Mac. It is published by OpenAppBox.

## The short version

PathDiff collects no data. It has no network access of its own, no sign-up, no analytics and no advertising. Your files and everything PathDiff knows about them stay on your Mac.

## What PathDiff can see

PathDiff runs in the macOS App Sandbox. It can only read and change the folders you choose in an open panel or drag onto its window. It cannot reach any other files on your Mac.

## What PathDiff stores on your Mac

To work, PathDiff keeps a few things inside its own app container on your Mac. None of it ever leaves your Mac.

| What | Why | How to remove it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Your settings | So PathDiff remembers your choices | Removed when you delete the app |
| Recent comparisons (folder locations and macOS access bookmarks) | To reopen comparisons and the Open Recent menu | File ▸ Open Recent Comparison ▸ Clear Menu |
| Remembered comparison results (file sizes, dates and whether files matched) | So refreshing or reopening a comparison only reads files that changed | Settings ▸ Comparison ▸ Clear |
| Sync History, with backups of files a change overwrote (up to 2 GB) | So you can undo or revert changes, even after relaunching | Sync History ▸ Clear History |

Deleting PathDiff removes all of it.

## What PathDiff sends

Nothing. PathDiff doesn't connect to the internet. Subscribing, renewals and checking the subscription go through the App Store, which is part of macOS and run by Apple.

The links to openappbox.com (in the Help menu, About PathDiff, the start screen and Settings) open the website in your web browser; that website has its own privacy policy.

## Crash reports

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in macOS (System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Analytics & Improvements), Apple may send OpenAppBox anonymous crash reports for PathDiff. These are collected by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, and you can turn this off at any time in the same place.

## Purchases

PathDiff's subscription is sold through the Mac App Store. Apple handles purchases, payment, renewals and refunds; OpenAppBox doesn't receive your payment details or your Apple Account information. PathDiff only asks the App Store, on your Mac, whether the subscription is active.

## Feedback

If you open an issue in this repository, what you write is public. Please don't include personal information or private files.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new date.
